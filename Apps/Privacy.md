# App Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

This policy covers any of my apps that link to this page.

## No data collected

These apps don't collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.

Anything the app needs to remember, such as your settings, is kept on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions

These apps only ask for the permissions they need to work. None of those permissions are used to collect or send data about you.

## App stores

If you install one of these apps from an app store such as Google Play, the store handles the download, updates and any data it collects as part of that, under its own privacy policy (for Google Play, [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). I don't receive any of it beyond the anonymous, aggregated install and crash statistics the store shows every developer.

## Children

These apps don't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If an app ever starts handling personal data, it will get its own policy before that happens.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at <https://github.com/Yazwh0/BitMagic/issues>.
